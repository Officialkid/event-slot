# Expansion Strategy

This section outlines a phased growth approach for extending EventSlot beyond initial markets while keeping operations stable.

## Geographic roadmap

| Phase | Focus |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 | Kenya operational depth |
| Phase 2 | East Africa expansion |
| Phase 3 | Pan-Africa playbook scaling |

## Country-specific considerations

| Market | Priority integration consideration |
|---|---|
| Kenya | M-Pesa alignment alongside existing billing stack |
| Nigeria | Flutterwave evaluation plus local payment preferences |
| Regional | local compliance and payment reliability per jurisdiction |

## Localization strategy

Planned localization includes:

- Swahili support
- additional regional language options based on demand
- culturally relevant onboarding and communication templates

## Partnership strategy

High-leverage partnerships include:

- universities and student communities
- churches and faith-based networks
- community organisations and NGOs
- SME ecosystems running recurring events

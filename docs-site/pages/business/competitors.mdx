# Competitor Analysis

This comparison maps EventSlot against common alternatives to clarify where it currently leads and where it still needs investment.

## Comparison table

| Capability | EventSlot | Google Forms | Eventbrite | Meetup | Luma |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity and waitlist automation | Strong | Weak | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Africa-first payment pathway | Strong (Paystack) | Weak | Weak | Weak | Weak |
| Organiser operations dashboard | Strong | Weak | Strong | Medium | Strong |
| Simplicity for slot-limited events | Strong | Medium | Medium | Medium | Strong |
| Marketplace discovery depth | Lower | None | Strong | Strong | Medium |

## Where EventSlot wins

- waitlist-first automation
- local-market payment alignment
- lightweight organiser workflow
- free-core usage model

## Where EventSlot is behind

- global brand awareness
- breadth of deep enterprise features
- mature marketplace network effects

## Positioning strategy

Position EventSlot as the pragmatic middle path between basic form tools and heavyweight ticketing marketplaces.

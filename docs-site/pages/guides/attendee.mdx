# Attendee Guide

> [!NOTE]
> Attendee registration in EventSlot is link-based. An attendee account is not required for standard event registration.

## Find and register for an event

1. Open the event link shared by organiser.
2. Fill required registration questions.
3. Submit registration.

## After registration

If confirmed immediately, you receive confirmation response and follow-up communication flow.

## If event is full

When capacity is full, your registration is still accepted but saved with waitlist status.

## Check waitlist position

Waitlist position is attached to your registration record and shown through status views tied to your registration link/context.

## Cancel registration

Cancellation can be handled from attendee registration management surfaces where enabled or by organiser actions on your registration record.

## What promotion looks like

When a slot opens through capacity increase, promoted attendees receive slot-confirmed communication and status update from waitlist to confirmed.

Expected user-visible signs:

- updated registration status
- email confirmation when email is available
- organiser-side attendee list update

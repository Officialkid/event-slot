# Multi-Campus Rollout

This guide helps institutions scale EventSlot operations without losing governance.

## Recommended Org Model

- Central platform admin team
- Campus-level organizer groups
- Shared policy baseline for privacy and attendance

## Rollout Phases

1. Pilot one campus with representative event formats.
2. Standardize templates and reporting structure.
3. Train local operators and support champions.
4. Expand region-by-region with phased SLAs.

## Governance Controls

- Use role-based permissions for organizer scopes.
- Require quarterly access reviews.
- Maintain centralized incident and compliance logs.
